The five development samples preserve their dataset license and upstream license references. These files do not grant rights beyond those held by their contributors.

The full development dataset is distributed separately on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/promptless-research/dogbench-dev/tree/f8e5dd2040414810cc79e8f77167c84a12d76932). Its immutable revision and source-file hashes are recorded in `data/release-reference.json`.
